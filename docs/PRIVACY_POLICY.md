## Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy describes how **FlashFits Technologies Private Limited** (“**FlashFits**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) accesses, collects, stores, processes, and protects your personal data when you interact with our platform. This policy applies to:

1. The **FlashFits Mobile Application** for iOS and Android;

3. Our on-demand quick-commerce fashion delivery services, including our doorstep **Try & Buy** experience; and
4. Any associated customer support, communications, or inquiries.

We have structured this policy to adhere to applicable privacy frameworks, including the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** (India), the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**, **Apple App Store Review Guidelines** (Guidelines 5.1.1 on Data Collection and Storage & 5.1.1(v) on Account Deletion), and **Google Play Developer Policies**.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use our services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to create your account, display nearby fashion stores, deliver apparel, facilitate doorstep trials, process payments, and provide customer support.

### 1.1 Identity & Contact Information
* **Full Name:** Used to identify you and personalize your account.
* **Mobile Phone Number:** Used as your primary account credential for secure One-Time Password (OTP) login authentication, delivery status updates, and courier delivery coordination.
* **Email Address:** Used for sending order confirmations, digital receipts, support communications, and administrative notices.

### 1.2 Delivery Addresses & Location Information
* **Saved Addresses:** Recipient name, complete street address, house/apartment number, landmarks, and PIN code.
* **Foreground Geolocation (When in Use):** When you grant location permission, we access your device’s approximate or precise GPS location **while the app is actively in use** to:
  * Identify and display nearby verified boutiques within delivery range (~5 km);
  * Automatically assist in populating your delivery address; and
  * Calculate delivery availability and estimated arrival times.
* *Important Note:* The customer mobile application does **not** track or collect your location in the background when the app is closed or minimized.

### 1.3 Order, Trial & Transaction Information
* **Order History:** Apparel items selected, sizes, quantities, prices, trial session timestamps, items purchased, and items returned on-the-spot.
* **Payment Records:** Selected payment method (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking), transaction status, and payment gateway transaction reference identifiers.
* *Payment Security:* All online payments are handled directly by our certified payment gateway partner, **Razorpay**. FlashFits does **not** collect, store, or process raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or banking passwords on our servers.

### 1.4 Device & Technical Information
* **Device Telemetry:** Device model, operating system (iOS/Android), unique device identifiers, and app version.
* **Push Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens (via Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) used strictly to deliver time-sensitive order updates, trial reminders, and dispatch alerts.

### 1.5 Photos & Customer Support Communications
* **Photos & Camera Access:** If you choose to upload images for returns, damaged item reports, or customer service queries, we access only the specific images you select with your permission.
* **Support Chats & Emails:** Inquiries and messages sent via in-app channels, WhatsApp Care (`+91 83838 23813`), or email (`flashfits.pvt@gmail.com`).

## 2. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information through:
1. **Direct Submissions:** Data you enter when creating an account, selecting delivery addresses, choosing clothing sizes, or messaging customer support.
2. **Device Permissions:** Foreground location access and camera/photo library access granted explicitly through iOS or Android permission prompts.
3. **App & Order Activity:** Automatic generation of order records, doorstep trial decisions (items kept vs. returned), and invoice records during platform use.
4. **Payment Gateway Callbacks:** Automated status notifications from Razorpay regarding transaction success, cancellation, or refund processing.

## 3. Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal information for the following commercial and operational purposes:
* **Account Setup & Security:** Authenticating phone numbers via OTP and managing user sessions.
* **Order Fulfillment:** Dispatching orders to local partner stores and coordinating doorstep deliveries.
* **Try & Buy Doorstep Service:** Managing trial items, logging on-the-spot returns, and calculating final payment amounts.
* **Payment & Refund Processing:** Coordinating payment verification and processing instant or bank refunds through Razorpay.
* **Customer Support:** Resolving queries regarding sizing, orders, trial rules, or returns via WhatsApp and email.
* **Fraud Prevention & Security:** Protecting user accounts against unauthorized access, payment fraud, and platform abuse.
* **Legal Compliance:** Complying with statutory requirements under Indian law, including commercial record-keeping and applicable tax regulations.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

### 5.1 What is Deleted Upon Request
When you delete your account (via the app or through a web request), we permanently remove the following from our active systems:
* Your profile, name, and email address;
* Your mobile phone login pairing and authentication tokens;
* All saved delivery addresses;
* Wishlists, carts, and app search history; and
* Device push notification identifiers.

### 5.2 Legally Required Retention
As permitted by Apple App Store and Google Play guidelines, certain information may be retained where required by applicable law:
* **Statutory Financial & Tax Records:** Completed order invoices and transaction receipts are retained for the period required by applicable Indian tax, accounting, and commercial regulations.
* **Dispute & Fraud Records:** Records strictly necessary to resolve pending payment disputes, chargebacks, or comply with anti-fraud obligations.
* *Isolation:* Retained records are kept in secure, restricted-access storage, detached from active user authentication, and are never used for marketing or commercial profiling.

## 6. How to Delete Your Account & Personal Data

In compliance with **Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v)** and **Google Play Policies**, FlashFits allows you to delete your account both from inside the app and through external web channels:

### Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Direct)
You can permanently delete your account directly inside the FlashFits mobile app:
1. Open the **FlashFits** app.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page (below the Log Out button).
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. When the confirmation prompt appears, select **Delete Permanently**.
6. Your account is immediately deactivated, your session ends, and your personal data is queued for automated removal.

## 8. Data Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:
* **Secure Communications:** All data transmitted between our mobile applications, website, and servers is encrypted in transit using modern HTTPS/TLS protocols.
* **Access Restrictions:** Access to database systems is strictly limited to authorized personnel with authenticated credentials on a need-to-know basis.
* **Token-Based Authentication:** User logins are secured using encrypted authentication tokens rather than storing raw passwords.
* **Payment Security:** Payments are routed directly through PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure provided by Razorpay.

While we take rigorous steps to safeguard your data, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.
